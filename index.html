01 — RESEARCH

What I'm working on

Evaluation and alignment for high-stakes domains, built and run on ETH's Euler HPC cluster.

Alignment LLM-as-a-Judge

FinRegQA-EU

Law, Economics & Data Science Group · Prof. Elliott Ash · 2026

DPO fine-tuning of Qwen3.5-4B on EBA/ESMA regulatory Q&A with five preference-corruption strategies.  Standard LLM-as-a-Judge evaluation rewards citation density as evidence of grounding and cannot detect when those citations are fabricated. Thus, we detect a blind spot that matters wherever answers must be verifiable.

Read the paper →
Benchmark Robustness

V-FiLLM — Verifiable Financial Reasoning

ETH AI Center × Apertus · Dr. Nino Antulov-Fantulin · 2026

A deterministic computation-tree benchmark with verifiable ground truth. Benchmarked eight models including Apertus-70B and Llama-3.3-70B, and stress-tested them against missing values, OCR lookalikes, prompt injection and cross-table contamination.

Read the paper →
Fairness DPO

Gated Graph-of-Gradients DPO

ETH AI Center · 2026

On PRISM, demographic groups don't explain DPO loss disparities — gradient-space groups do. We built a graph-of-gradients reweighting method on that finding.

VLMs Evaluation

Relational Reasoning in VLMs

Evaluated state-of-the-art vision-language models on spatial, semantic and compositional relations; mapped failure modes and proposed targeted benchmarks.
